02 / ABOUT.TXT

✳

Curious by default.
Making to understand.

I’m a game design student in Finland with a background in design and communication. I enjoy shaping ideas, exploring how people think and feel, and connecting research, visuals, and storytelling into coherent experiences.

This archive collects selected projects and experiments across game design, visual identity, UX, and creative research. It’s a work in progress — much like the person behind it.

Moi — Everyday Finnish

GAME DESIGN / LANGUAGE LEARNING

A mobile game concept that helps learners practise Finnish through short, everyday tasks in a warm imagined town. Single-choice dialogue reduces decision pressure, while a sticker notebook helps players revisit vocabulary and grammar.
